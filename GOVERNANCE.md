# Governance

Open ASPM is currently maintained by the repository owner. Technical decisions
are made in public issues, discussions, and pull requests whenever they do not
involve embargoed security information.

Maintainers are responsible for project direction, releases, reviews, security
response, and enforcement of the Code of Conduct. Contributors who establish a
record of constructive, sustained participation may be invited to become
maintainers. The maintainer model and decision process will evolve as the
community grows.

Security-sensitive decisions may be discussed privately until coordinated
disclosure is safe.
